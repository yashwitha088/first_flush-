Methodology and safety

Risk is a relative prioritization estimate using documented weights for dry period, rainfall, paved surface, traffic, construction, waste, blockage, and water-body proximity. It is not a laboratory water-quality measurement.

Physical interventions and sampling must only be performed by trained or authorized personnel. Weather and field records show their source, mode, freshness, and verification state.
